DON'T DIE

ONE TAP. ONE RULE.

A ball of light races down a neon corridor. Every tap turns it 90°. Touch a wall, a saw or a bomb and it's over.

The game

  • 20 levels in 4 worlds, and HARD MODE after level 20
  • Endless Survival that keeps speeding up
  • A new Daily Run every day, the same track for everyone
  • Skins with super powers: tap 4 times fast
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking, works offline

Support

Found a bug, an idea, or a level you love to hate? Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have.

Lost progress? Progress is saved on your iPhone only. It survives updates, but deleting the app deletes it.

No sound? Check the sound button at the top of the menu, and your iPhone's volume and silent switch.

Reminders can be turned on or off in Settings > Notifications > DON'T DIE.
