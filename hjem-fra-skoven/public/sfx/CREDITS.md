# Credits: sound effects and music in `public/sfx/`

Thanks to: Kenney (kenney.nl), the OpenGameArt.org contributors listed below, and the U.S. National Park Service (Yellowstone Sound Library and Natural Sounds gallery) / U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service.
